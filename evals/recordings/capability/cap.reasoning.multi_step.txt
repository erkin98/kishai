    $$60\,\text{minutes} \times 60\,\text{seconds/minute} = 3,600\,\text{seconds}$$

    $$10,800\,\text{requests} \times \$0.002/\text{request}$$

    $$\$0.006/\text{second} \times 3,600\,\text{seconds} = \$21.60$$
